# Aerosol Masking (the Faustian Bargain)

## Description

Anthropogenic aerosols — sulfate above all — cool the planet by reflecting sunlight directly and by brightening clouds ([[twomey-effect]], [[cloud-adjustments]]). This cooling has been hiding a fraction of committed greenhouse warming since the industrial era; cleaning the air for health reasons collects the debt. Hansen's "Faustian bargain" framing is now standard: every aerosol reduction converts hidden warming into observed warming.[^1] The magnitude of the mask is the biggest single uncertainty in anthropogenic forcing,[^2] which is why the same evidence supports climate sensitivities from ~3°C to Hansen's 4.8°C per doubling.[^3]

The [[imo-2020-regulation]] is the cleanest real-world test of the mask ever run: a known, dated, quantified aerosol removal over dark ocean. Observationally informed estimates of the shipping slice of the mask ranged up to −1.0 W/m² of global low-cloud forcing pre-2020;[^4] the measured post-2020 unmasking is +0.07 to +0.2 W/m² ([[imo2020-forcing-estimates]]). For scale, that one regulation equals about half the forcing from the decline of *all* anthropogenic aerosols since the late 20th century.[^5]

The concept generalizes beyond ships: Chinese SO₂ cleanup since ~2013, coal plant scrubbers, and diesel standards are all withdrawals from the same account. If [[manshausen-2022-invisible-ship-tracks]] is right that cloud responses were underestimated, the mask — and the warming still owed — is larger than assessed.[^6]
